The firm

AGGA

Adv. Gaurav Goyal & Associates is a full-service law firm based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, providing legal representation and advisory services across diverse areas of law.

The firm’s practice includes Criminal Law, Civil and Commercial Disputes, NRI Estate Management Service, Land and Revenue Laws, Intellectual Property Rights, Labour and Employment Laws, Information Technology and Cyber Law, Environmental Laws, Arbitration and Mediation, and Recovery and Enforcement matters.

The firm represents individuals, businesses and institutions before courts, tribunals and other statutory and quasi-judicial authorities.
